Realistic forest kit - built by tools/blender_forest/ (see its README.md).

All photographic sources are CC0 1.0 (public domain) from Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com:
  textures: forest_leaves_02, forrest_ground_01, forest_ground_04, bark_brown_02, pine_bark,
            tree_bark_03
  models:   shrub_04 (leaf scans), fern_02, grass_medium_01, tree_stump_01, dead_tree_trunk,
            dry_branches_medium_01, rock_moss_set_01, rock_moss_set_02

Tree, bush, fern and grass geometry, the leaf / needle sprays and all packing are generated by
the scripts in tools/blender_forest/.
