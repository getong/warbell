Tree, bush, fern and grass geometry, the leaf / needle sprays, the structures / wayside models
and all packing are generated by the scripts in tools/blender_forest/.

Cloud sky (assets/sky/cloud_noise.png, the high cloud deck of src/clouds.rs + shaders/clouds.wgsl):
            original procedural bake, no third-party data: tileable Perlin-Worley shape /
            inverted-Worley detail / Perlin coverage noise generated by tools/bake_cloud_noise.py
            (CC0 1.0, like the rest of the generated assets)

Rock-range heightmap (assets/terrain/dolomites_sella_320.r16 — the rock massif's shape, sampled by
src/alpine/terrain_massif.rs):
  Real elevation of the Dolomites around Passo Sella (Sella group, Sassolungo/Langkofel, the head
  of Val Gardena and Val di Fassa; ~13.5 km square centred ≈46.53 N, 11.78 E), from the
  "Terrain Tiles" dataset on AWS Open Data (terrarium encoding, zoom 12, tiles 2181–2183 /
  1447–1449): https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
  Terrain Tiles attribution: "© Mapzen, and others" — sources include SRTM and GMTED (NASA/USGS,
  public domain), EU-DEM (Copernicus, © European Union), ETOPO1 (NOAA). See
  https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
  Decoded h = R·256 + G + B/256 − 32768, 512×512 px crop box-filtered to 320×320, local-relief
  unsharp mask (σ 2.5 samples, ×0.8), normalised min..max → 0..65535, raw little-endian u16
  (tools/terrain/bake_dem.py).
